\chapter{Conclusion}

As part of this project, we investigated methods for securing PhantomOS snapshots. To this end, we modeled the various scenarios of computer operation, classified them by access type and user trust, and selected a set of supported scenarios from among them. Based on file\_vault, we created se\_vault, which is responsible for encrypting snapshots. In addition, we made changes to other components to ensure that se\_vault functions correctly. As a result, se\_vault provides support for the selected scenarios.

The tasks set out in this research were accomplished, with one reservation regarding performance: the modeling and selection of scenarios proceeded according to plan, and snapshot security was successfully ensured and experimentally verified; however, this came at a significant performance cost.

In the future, this work should contribute to the study of the security of persistent operating systems in general and the PhantomOS port on Genode in particular. Work of this kind should have a positive impact on the capabilities of such operating systems and may facilitate their faster adoption.

This line of research has not yet been exhausted. Further work will focus on identifying the causes of the high performance overhead and resolving it as well as optimizing the developed component and expanding its scope of application within the context of this operating system. In addition, other computer usage scenarios may be considered and supported, and the current one may be expanded.
